What makes it different · सहा नवकल्पना
REAL MODEL
01 · Pashu Lens
📸

AI identifies the animal from a photo

EfficientNetV2 trained on 50 Indian cattle & buffalo breeds, with a subject gate that refuses non-animals and a quality gate that asks for a retake. Registration in one tap — aligned to Bharat Pashudhan's image-based classification agenda.

EPIDEMIOLOGY
02 · Outbreak Radar
📡

Statistical cluster detection, not a dot map

Space-time scan with Poisson p-values flags a village cluster days before deaths pile up, names the suspected disease, raises One Health notices for zoonoses and auto-creates owned response tasks.

PRESCRIPTIVE
03 · What-if Planner
🔮

"Ring-vaccinate today → how many cases prevented?"

A transparent SEIR model on the village graph animates the next 7 days and compares no action vs ring vaccination, with doses needed — the decision a Collector actually has to make.

CONTAINMENT
04 · Health Passport + Movement Permit
🪪

A QR that stops a sick village selling into a clean one

Every animal gets a scannable passport: vaccinations, treatments, milk-withdrawal countdown and a live movement permit computed from the Radar — enforced at checkposts and markets.

INCLUSION
05 · Pashu Mitra voice
🗣️

Speak in Marathi. It answers in Marathi.

Voice symptom capture, triage result and the advisory read aloud, IVR-1962 keypad path and an SMS codec — a farmer who cannot read or has no data is still a first-class reporter.

INCENTIVE
06 · Reporting pays
💰

The case record is the compensation claim

Farmers hide outbreaks because reporting triggers culling. Here a death report becomes a one-tap compensation claim officers approve in the portal — earlier reporting, earlier detection.
