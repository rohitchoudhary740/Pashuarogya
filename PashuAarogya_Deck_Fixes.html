03 · Four additions that win marksCopy is written — paste it in

What no other team will be able to show

1 · Put the PS's own outcomes on the slide, with a number

The problem statement names six expected outcomes. Most teams promise them; nobody measures them. We now can — the platform records when the animal fell ill, not just when it was reported.

Reporting delay: 6.4 days → 1.5 days. Median onset-to-report, measured inside the platform across 237 cases as adoption grows.

Goes on slide 5 (Impact). It answers "how do you know it works?" — the question most finalists cannot answer. Say plainly that the pilot data is simulated; the measurement is real and runs live.

2 · Stop looking like a rival. Be the layer on top.

The comparison table marks us green on all ten rows. All-green tables read as self-serving and invite attack.

1962 treats one animal at a time. Bharat Pashudhan holds the lifetime record. Neither turns those into village-level early warning — that is our layer. We read their data by Pashu Aadhaar; we never ask a farmer to register an animal twice.

Then deliberately concede two rows where they win — the national animal registry, and 1962's MVU fleet. A team that concedes something is trusted on everything else.

3 · Lead with the safety rule — it is missing entirely

In a MedTech/HealthTech theme, the liability question is coming. Answer it before it is asked.

The system outputs a suspected category, a severity band and a route — never a diagnosis. Diagnosis and treatment remain with the registered veterinarian. That rule is enforced in the architecture, not in a disclaimer.

One line on slide 2 or 5. It converts the biggest risk in the theme into a design credential.

4 · Frame the disease count — and offer a live demo

"17+ diseases" sounds thin on its own. Against the statutory list it is a roadmap.

13 of the 83 diseases notifiable under the Prevention & Control of Infectious and Contagious Diseases in Animals Act, 2009, plus 4 production diseases farmers actually ring about — mastitis, black quarter, mange, enterotoxaemia. The knowledge base is hot-reloadable: adding a disease is 20 lines of YAML and one click.

Then offer to add a disease live, on the judges' request. No other team can do that, and it proves the department would own the rules, not us.

The 83-disease list is in the repo: backend/rules/scheduled_diseases.yaml4 / 7
