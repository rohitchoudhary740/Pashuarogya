01 · The answer in one pageRead this if you read nothing else

Right direction. Three strategic gaps. Five fixes that matter most.

Every capability the PS lists is at least partly there. The judges from the department will probe three places. If we fix those, most of the "partly met" items become "met" as a side effect.

✅ What we got right

  • Real outbreak detection: a space-time scan statistic that gives a p-value, not a colour-coded guess.
  • Closed loop: report → triage → sample → lab → cluster → task → compensation.
  • Officials' side is strong: radar, what-if forecast, SITREP, history, live sync, CSV export.
  • Low-friction reporting: voice in Hindi/Marathi, icons, offline queue.
  • Food safety: milk/meat withdrawal alerts after treatment. The PS doesn't ask for this; judges will like it.

⚠️ The three strategic gaps

  1. Outcomes are unmeasured. The PS names 6 expected outcomes. We measure 1. The report form never asks when the animal fell ill (the API hard-codes onset_date = today), so we can't show reduced reporting time.
  2. We pitch as a standalone app. The real overlap is small: Bharat Pashudhan keeps records for tagged animals, and the 1962 Farmer App offers home remedies for some diseases (checked by our team). But a standalone pitch invites the question "why not just use 1962?"
  3. Advisories stay inside the app. There's no SMS, push, or voice broadcast. Outbreak advisories also reach only the epicentre village, not the whole zone.

🎯 Five fixes, in order

  1. Onset date + Outcome Scoreboard: live KPIs for all 6 PS outcomes. Small.
  2. Reposition + 1962 Signal Bridge: take 1962 call tickets and Bharat Pashudhan records as inputs. Medium.
  3. Zone broadcast with acknowledgement: SMS/voice to every village in the risk zone. Medium.
  4. LSD-2022 replay: show on the real Maharashtra outbreak timeline that we detect earlier. Medium.
  5. Herd/flock mode + death report: a one-tap mortality report and flock-level poultry records. Medium.
The one sentence to change in our pitch. Stop saying "a new app for farmers and vets." Say: "PashuAarogya is the surveillance brain that sits on top of 1962 and Bharat Pashudhan. It turns their one-animal-at-a-time treatment records into village-level early warning." That answers "why not use 1962?" before anyone asks it.

03 · Expected Outcomes"Expected outcomes include…"

The PS promises six outcomes. We can currently show one.

This is our biggest hole, and it's cheap to fix. Judges score impact, and impact means numbers that move. Most of the timestamps we need are already in the database. We just never compute the durations.

Expected outcomeStatusWhere we standThe KPI to show (and where the data already lives)
Reduced reporting timeNOT MET Can't be measured: onset_date is hard-coded to today (main.py:308) and the seeded data sets onset = report date. The delay is always zero. Median onset → report, in hours, trended weekly and split by channel (app / field / IVR). Needs the onset picker first.
Earlier outbreak identificationNOT MET We detect clusters, but we claim "earlier" without evidence. There's no baseline to compare against. Detection lead-time: Outbreak.detected_at vs the first lab-confirmed case in the zone. Plus the LSD-2022 replay (innovation C).
Improved vaccination coveragePARTLY An overall 12-month coverage % is shown (main.py:538), and camps can be scheduled. Coverage vs target per village, due and overdue (defaulter) lists, and coverage gained per camp.
Faster treatment and containmentNOT MET The timestamps exist (reported_at, collected_at, result_at, Task.done_at), but no durations are computed. Report → first vet action, measured against Maharashtra's own 1-hour callback norm. Sample → result turnaround. Cluster → ring vaccination.
Lower mortality and productivity lossPARTLY Deaths in the last 7 days are counted (main.py:527), with no rate and no trend. Case-fatality rate by disease, deaths per 1,000 animals, and an estimated ₹ milk loss per outbreak.
Stronger evidence-based planningMET SITREP, the what-if ring-vaccination forecast, historical trends and CSV exports. Already demoable. Add "decisions taken from this SITREP" to close the loop.
Found in the code: a KPI we promised ourselves and never built
main.py:539 has the comment # median report->awareness lag in hours (reported_at vs onset) directly above the return statement. The metric was never implemented. It's the first outcome in the PS, and we'd already planned to build it.
